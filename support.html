Common help

Forgot your password?

Open the Mau Daily login screen and select Forgot Password. Enter the email address linked to your account.

Change your profile

Open Profile → Edit profile to update your display name or username.

Manage what friends can see

Open Profile → Privacy to control mood and sleep sharing. Open My routines to mark individual routines as private.

Block or report someone

Open that person's Mau profile to use the block or report options. Blocked users can be reviewed from Profile → Blocked users.

Delete your account

Open Profile → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. Account deletion is permanent.
