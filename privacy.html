Privacy Policy

How Mau Daily handles information used to provide the app.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mau Daily collects and processes, why it is used, how sharing works, and the choices available to you.

For privacy questions, contact maudailyapp@gmail.com.

2. Information used by Mau Daily

Account information

When you create an account, Mau Daily uses information such as your display name, username, email address and account identifier.

Routine and check-in information

The app stores information you choose to enter, including routines, routine completion, mood, sleep and daily history.

Social and safety information

The app processes friend requests and friendships, blocked-user relationships, and reports submitted through the in-app reporting feature.

Technical information

Mau Daily uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication and cloud data storage. Those services may process technical information necessary to provide, secure and maintain the service in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

3. How information is used

  • to create and authenticate your account;
  • to store and display your routines and daily history;
  • to enable friend requests and accepted friendships;
  • to show permitted daily information to accepted friends;
  • to provide blocking and reporting features;
  • to maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot the service.

4. What friends can see

Mau Daily is designed so that friend-only information is shared only with accepted friends. You can independently control whether your mood and sleep are shared. You can also mark individual routines as private.

Private routines are not included in the friend-shared daily view. Turning mood or sleep sharing off hides that information from friends while keeping it available in your own private history.

5. What Mau Daily does not collect for the current version

  • No precise GPS location is collected by the app.
  • No advertising profile is created by Mau Daily.
  • Mau Daily does not sell your personal information.
  • The current version does not require profile-photo uploads.

6. Service providers

Mau Daily uses Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide account authentication and cloud-based app functionality. Information needed for those functions may therefore be processed by Google as a service provider.

7. Data retention and account deletion

Your account information and app content are generally retained while your account remains active so that Mau Daily can provide the service.

You can permanently delete your account from within the app using Profile → Delete account. The deletion flow is designed to remove your authentication account and associated Mau Daily profile, routine, daily-history, friendship, blocked-user and shared-check-in data.

Safety or abuse reports may be retained after account deletion where reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate misuse, maintain service integrity, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. Such information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

8. Your choices

You can manage sharing settings inside Mau Daily, remove friends, block or unblock users, and delete your account. You may also contact us to ask a privacy question or request assistance concerning your account or personal information.

9. Security

Mau Daily uses authenticated access and Firestore security rules to restrict access to app data. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps within the service architecture to limit unauthorized access.

10. Children

Mau Daily is not designed as a service directed specifically to young children. If we learn that personal information has been provided in circumstances that require deletion under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Mau Daily's features, service providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
